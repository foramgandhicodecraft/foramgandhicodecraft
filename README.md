### 🔬 Research
- 📄 **Mitigating Gender Bias in LLMs through Prompt-Level Fairness Intervention**
  Applied prompt-level debiasing strategies (counterfactual rewriting, role-neutral framing) to reduce gender bias scores by ~96% across multiple LLMs. *(Under Dr. Rupa Mehta)*

- 📄 **Explainable AI for EEG-Based Seizure Detection and Cross-Patient Generalization**
  Building an XAI pipeline on EEG data for seizure detection, followed by post-hoc feature attribution (SHAP/saliency maps) to identify discriminative channels and temporal patterns before and after cross-patient generalization. *(Under Prof. Abhilasha Chaudhury)*

- Beyond research, I build full-stack and AI-powered systems spanning MERN, Django, Flask, GenAI integrations, computer vision (OpenCV), and analytics dashboards (Power BI).
